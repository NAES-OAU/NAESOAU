Privacy Notice

NAESOAU collects your name, registration or matric number, date of birth (no year), phone number, email, level, image and payment receipts. We use them only to manage membership, confirm dues and issue receipts.

Only NAESOAU executives can see this information. Images and receipts are stored privately. Speak Up messages do not store your name.

To block spam and abuse we also record the internet (IP) address used for registrations and receipts, and for wrong attempts to look up a payment. For Speak Up messages the IP address is erased after one hour.

To correct or delete your details, contact an executive or send a Speak Up message with your contact.

If you tick “Keep me signed in on this device”, your matric number and WhatsApp number are saved in this browser only, so you do not have to type them again. Use Sign out at the top of any page to remove them. Do not tick it on a shared phone or computer.

Privacy Notice

NAES-OAU collects your name, registration or matric number, date of birth (no year), phone number, email, level, image and payment receipts. We use them only to manage membership, confirm dues and issue receipts.

Only NAES-OAU executives can see this information. Images and receipts are stored privately. Speak Up messages do not store your name.

To block spam and abuse we also record the internet (IP) address used for registrations and receipts, and for wrong attempts to look up a payment. For Speak Up messages the IP address is erased after one hour.

Executives sign in with their own accounts, and each account has a role that limits what it can see and do. Actions on records, such as edits, deletions and payment approvals, are logged with the executive's email.

To correct or delete your details, contact an executive or send a Speak Up message with your contact.

If you tick “Remember my matric number and WhatsApp number on this device”, they are saved in this browser only, so you do not have to type them again. Open the menu on any page and tap Forget to remove them. Do not tick it on a shared phone or computer.

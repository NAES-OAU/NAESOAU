Welcome to NAES-OAU

The National Association of Entrepreneurship Students, Obafemi Awolowo University. Until 2024 it was called the Association of Entrepreneurship Students (AES).

NAES-OAU is run by students within the Institute for Entrepreneurship and Development Studies (IFEDS). It gives entrepreneurship students a place to sharpen their business skills, find mentors and team up on ventures, and it helps members turn ideas into real businesses.

Visit the Student Association Site

For events, workshops and mentorship, the association's own site is the best place to look.

What We Do

WorkshopsSkill-building sessions on starting and running a business.
Pitch CompetitionsPresent your business idea and get feedback on it.
MentorshipLearn from people who have built something before you.
Networking and Practical ExperienceMeet other student entrepreneurs and try ideas in real situations.

Vision and Mission

VisionTo be the leading student-driven association on the OAU campus, building a culture of entrepreneurship, innovation and leadership, and a strong network of young entrepreneurs who drive change at home and beyond.
MissionTo represent and support entrepreneurship students through events, workshops, mentorship and advocacy, so they build skills, launch student-led ventures, and grow an ecosystem of innovation, leadership and social responsibility.

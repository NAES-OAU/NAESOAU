Who We Are

A community built around possibility

NAES-OAU brings together students of entrepreneurship within the Institute for Entrepreneurship and Development Studies (IFEDS). Formerly known as the Association of Entrepreneurship Students (AES), the association provides a space to develop business skills, connect with mentors, collaborate with peers and take ideas towards real-world impact.

Our Direction

What guides us

Our Vision

To be the leading student-driven association on the OAU campus, building a culture of entrepreneurship, innovation and leadership, and a strong network of young entrepreneurs who drive change at home and beyond.

Our Mission

To represent and support entrepreneurship students through events, workshops, mentorship and advocacy, so they build skills, launch student-led ventures, and grow an ecosystem of innovation, leadership and social responsibility.

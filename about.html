OUR JOURNEY

Our Story

From a small beginning to a growing community of entrepreneurship students.

  1. 2017: AES Begins

    Five students, one male and four female, found that their first department placements did not match their qualifications. They came together and laid the groundwork for IFEDS at OAU and for the Association of Entrepreneurship Students. IFEDS was the smallest institute on campus at the time.

  2. Growth

    More students, including Direct Entry students, joined. AES grew into a hub for creativity and entrepreneurial skills.

  3. 2024: AES Becomes NAES-OAU

    Under President Oyewole Samuel Segun, the association rebranded as the National Association of Entrepreneurship Students, OAU. Past presidents Badmus Taiwo Oluwaseun and Williams Olasubomi Samuel approved the change, which reflects a wider aim of uniting entrepreneurship students under one national body.

  4. Today

    NAES-OAU focuses on hands-on experience, partnerships and capacity-building programmes for its members.
